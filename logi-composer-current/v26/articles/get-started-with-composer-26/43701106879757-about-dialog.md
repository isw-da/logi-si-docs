# About Dialog

The About dialog identifies the version of Composer you are using and provides a link to the terms of service. Access by selecting **About**  in the [main menu](https://logi-composer-v26.insightsoftware.com/hc/en-us/articles/43701160499853-The-Main-Menu).

Log out of the product: Select **Log out**.

View the Terms of Service by selecting the Terms of Service link.
